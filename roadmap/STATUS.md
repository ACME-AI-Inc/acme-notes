# Roadmap Status

Last updated: **2026-11-12**

Individual PRDs are the source of truth for tasks and evidence.

## Status Legend

- `Draft`: requirements or approach are being shaped.
- `Ready`: the stage is ready for implementation.
- `In progress`: implementation has started.
- `Blocked`: progress needs a decision or dependency.
- `Verifying`: implementation exists and acceptance is being checked.
- `Complete`: acceptance has supporting evidence.
- `Superseded`: a newer plan replaces this one.
